## 📌 Project Overview
Corporate research and competitive intelligence frequently encounter outdated, conflicting, or fragmented organizational data across public domains. 

This project executes an end-to-end secondary research workflow:
1. **Corporate Structure Analysis:** Deconstructing internal operational and engineering hierarchies across 5 North American companies spanning FinTech, InsurTech SaaS, Industrial Packaging, Defense Electronics, and Architectural Lighting.
2. **Executive Command Hierarchy Modeling:** Classifying operational authority tiers from C-Suite executives down through Senior Directors and Plant General Managers.
3. **Multi-Source Cross-Verification:** Cross-referencing professional directories against corporate disclosures, SEC filings, and specialized trade publications to detect executive turnover and structural shifts.
4. **Structured Analytical Reporting:** Delivering an 8-sheet structured Excel model (`Gagan_Dhanapune_DataAnalyst.xlsx`) with integrated verification tracking and source attribution.

## 🛠️ Research Methodology & Analytical Techniques
* **Advanced Boolean Query Design:** Precision search operators targeting corporate hierarchies, subsidiaries, and functional divisions.
* **Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT):** Extracting qualitative business intelligence from public filings, corporate portals, and industry media.
* **Critical Data Verification & Anomaly Detection:** Auditing records against official SEC disclosures and verified announcements to identify executive departures, interim appointments, and geographic ambiguities.
* **Automated Data Modeling (`openpyxl`):** Scripted generation of structured, color-coded Excel deliverables featuring hyperlinks and audit status flags.
